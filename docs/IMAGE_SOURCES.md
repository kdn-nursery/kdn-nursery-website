# Image sources

No owner photographs were in the project. The pictures below are species photographs from Wikimedia Commons, resized to fit inside 1600 pixels and saved as WebP in `public/plants/`. They were not cropped. They are not photographs of Kaviyadevan Nursery Garden.

CC BY and CC BY-SA images stay under those licences. The page shows the photographer and licence next to the photograph. CC BY-SA copies on this site remain CC BY-SA.
